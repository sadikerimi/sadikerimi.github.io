HepaData: Liver Disease Prediction

2026 · BBF304E · team project
scikit-learn5×2 cross-validationWilcoxon testsSMOTEPermutation importance

An end-to-end classification study on the Indian Liver Patient Dataset (583 patients, 570 after cleaning). We compared Logistic Regression, SVM, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting and an MLP with nested tuning, 5×2 cross-validation and statistical significance tests, then ran ablations, class-weighting and SMOTE experiments, learning curves and a multi-seed robustness check.

The dataset is 71% positive, so accuracy alone was misleading: Gradient Boosting and Random Forest both reached 72.8% test accuracy, but Gradient Boosting caught only 15% of healthy patients. We selected Random Forest on balanced accuracy and MCC instead.

Market Reactions to Turkish Corporate Disclosures (KAP)

in progress
PyTorchBERTurkTabiBERT (8k context)TF-IDF + LightGBMEvent study

Deep learning course project with two teammates. We are building a 2022–2026 dataset of KAP disclosures from BIST50 companies, including attachment text, and predicting abnormal, market-adjusted price reactions over 1 to 90 trading days by combining disclosure text with price history.

My part is the text side: the annotation guideline and tool, fine-tuning BERTurk against the long-context TabiBERT on about 2,000 manually labeled disclosures, and the TF-IDF + LightGBM and BERTurk baselines.
